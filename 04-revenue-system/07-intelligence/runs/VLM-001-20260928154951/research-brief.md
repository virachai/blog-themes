# Research Brief

- Mission: VLM-001
- Signal: SIG-001
- Issue: Define the real user problem before drafting
- Thesis: An evidence-backed editorial thesis should create a more useful asset than topic-first drafting
- Audience: general
- Problem: Unclear user problem causes low-value production
- Evidence plan: Use primary or authoritative sources where available
- Value class: asset
- Value path: organic discovery and durable usefulness

## Evidence ledger

### Observation 1
- Observation: Google Search Central says its automated ranking systems are designed to prioritize helpful, reliable information created to benefit people, and its self-assessment asks whether content serves an intended audience and helps readers achieve their goal.
- Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Date checked: 2026-09-28
- Claim supported: A content process should define the intended audience and user goal before drafting so usefulness can be assessed against the real reader rather than search-engine attraction alone.
- Uncertainty: Google's guidance describes what its systems seek to reward; it does not establish that every audience-first process will produce higher traffic or a causal effect on rankings.

### Observation 2
- Observation: Nielsen Norman Group describes a user-need statement as an actionable problem statement capturing who the user is, what they need, and why the need matters, and recommends grounding it in qualitative research before generating solutions.
- Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/user-need-statements/
- Date checked: 2026-09-28
- Claim supported: Explicitly stating the user, need, and goal before producing the solution helps keep work focused on the problem rather than prematurely locking onto an output.
- Uncertainty: This is UX research guidance rather than a controlled study of editorial publishing outcomes; it supports the process principle but does not quantify its effect on content performance.

## Editorial decision

- Thesis confirmed / revised / rejected: confirmed with qualification
- Reason: The evidence supports identifying the intended audience, user need, and goal before drafting. It does not prove a direct causal relationship between this process and organic traffic, so traffic remains a later measured outcome.
- Next action: Build the evidence-backed asset package, then run editorial/release gates without publishing automatically.
