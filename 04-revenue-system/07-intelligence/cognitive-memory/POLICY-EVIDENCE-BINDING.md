# Policy Evidence Binding

Stage 43 preserves a traceable chain from trusted learning to policy consideration:

`TRUSTED → ELIGIBLE → EVIDENCE_BOUND → POLICY PROPOSAL INPUT`

An evidence binding is not a policy. It is an explicit lineage record that a downstream policy proposal may consume.

Required lineage:
- eligible knowledge id;
- policy scope;
- intended purpose;
- claim;
- evidence reference.

Only ELIGIBLE knowledge may be bound.
