# Knowledge Eligibility

Stage 42 separates trust from operational eligibility.

TRUSTED means the learning survived the Stage 40 validation gate and explicit Stage 41 promotion. It does not mean every downstream use is authorized.

Lifecycle:

`TRUSTED → CANDIDATE → ELIGIBLE`

Eligibility is scoped by:
- target scope;
- intended purpose;
- evidence reference;
- explicit review.

An ELIGIBLE record is permission for downstream policy consideration, not policy creation or activation.
